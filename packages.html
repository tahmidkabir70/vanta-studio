GOOD TO KNOW

Before we
get started.

Some common questions about packages and project planning.

Are the package prices fixed?

No. Pricing is currently provided through a custom quotation. Final cost depends on scope, complexity, content and integrations.

Can I request a custom package?

Yes. You can describe your requirements and request a combination of deliverables suited to your project.

Does a package include hosting or a domain?

Not by default. Hosting, domain registration, paid services and third-party subscriptions should be discussed and quoted separately.

When does work begin?

Work should begin only after the scope, deliverables, timeline, responsibilities and payment terms have been agreed.
